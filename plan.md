# SynapseBridge AI — Reverse Mentorship Platform

## Design Direction
- **Accent Color:** Indigo-600 (#4F46E5) with violet highlights
- **Surface Style:** White cards with subtle borders on slate-50 background
- **Typography:** Inter font, strong hierarchy with semibold headings
- **Sidebar:** Light sidebar (white bg, border-right, indigo active indicators)
- **Aesthetic:** Clean, modern, Linear/Stripe-inspired — flat surfaces, minimal chrome, generous spacing
- **Icons:** Lucide icons throughout
